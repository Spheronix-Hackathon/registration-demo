1. Eligibility and Registration

  • Participants must provide authentic and verifiable registration details. Any submission of false information may result in immediate disqualification.
  • For team mode registrations, the team size must consist of a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 5 members, including the Team Leader.
  • Registration is considered confirmed only upon successful verification of the participation payment.

2. Code of Conduct and Originality

  • All code, designs, and project submissions must be original work created entirely during the official hackathon timeframe, unless the use of specific open-source libraries is explicitly permitted by the organizers.

  • The organizing committee reserves the right to audit code repositories, commit logs, and participant identification details to ensure compliance and fair play.
