Hackathon Participation Instructions

Please read the following guidelines carefully before participating.

Registration Guidelines

  • Complete the registration form with accurate and verifiable information.
  • Ensure all required fields marked with an asterisk (*) are populated.
  • Utilize a valid email address via Google OAuth for authentication and primary communication.
  • A payment confirmation and official receipt will be emailed upon successful registration.

Team Participation

  • For team participation, the designated Team Leader must initiate and complete the registration process.
  • Team size is strictly limited to 5 members (1 Team Leader and up to 4 additional members).
  • The Team Leader must provide the Full Name, Email, Mobile, and Roll Number for each member.
  • Institutional details (College and Branch) are inherited from the Team Leader and apply globally to the team.
  • Ensure your team name is unique and adheres to professional guidelines.

Project Submission

  • Submit your project before the deadline.
  • Ensure the project is original and developed within the hackathon period.
  • Follow all competition rules and submission guidelines.
  • Make sure your code is well-documented and clean.

Important Rules

  • Plagiarism is strictly prohibited and will result in disqualification.
  • All participants must adhere to the code of conduct.
  • Late submissions will not be accepted.
  • Decision of the judges will be final.
